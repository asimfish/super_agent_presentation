# Absorbed reporting standards

This registry names the external reporting standards and research findings the
framework draws on, the core rule taken from each, and where that rule is encoded
so the mapping stays auditable. Encoding locations are the canonical statement;
this file is documentation, not an additional contract layer. Domain-template
provenance (ML, RL, embodied, VLA, academic talks) lives separately in
[TEMPLATE-SOURCES.md](TEMPLATE-SOURCES.md).

| Standard / source | Core rule absorbed | Encoded in |
|---|---|---|
| BLUF (US Army AR 25-50 correspondence standard) | The first sentence carries the conclusion or requested action; background follows. | `references/modes/concise-answer.md` required shape; `outcome-not-first` and `process-diary-opening` audit warnings; core-contract reader contract rule 1 |
| Inverted pyramid (journalism; NN/g "Inverted Pyramid: Writing for Comprehension") | Order content most-important-first at page, section, paragraph, and sentence level; front-load headings and first words. | Semantic order sections of every mode file; `outcome-not-first` audit warning |
| Minto Pyramid Principle / SCQA (Barbara Minto) | One governing thought stated as a complete claim, supported by two to four non-overlapping reasons; a reader of only the top layer holds the full picture. | `references/modes/decision-brief.md` decision discipline; `assets/templates/executive-onepager.md` |
| SBAR (IHI/AHRQ/WHO handoff standard) | Situation, background, assessment (explicit judgment, separate from data), recommendation with time bound and contingency. | `references/modes/incident-update.md` semantic order; `assets/templates/sbar-handoff.md` |
| Google SRE postmortem culture (SRE Book ch. 15) | Blameless systemic analysis; quantified impact; timeline; what went well / wrong / where we got lucky; owned, tracked, typed action items. | `references/modes/postmortem.md`; `assets/templates/postmortem.md` |
| US federal plain language guidelines (plainlanguage.gov, OPM; ISO 24495-1) | 15-20 word average sentences; one idea per paragraph; short paragraphs; common words; descriptive unique headings; lists with lead-ins, kept shallow. | `long-sentence`, `dense-paragraph`, `deep-list-nesting`, `generic-heading` audit warnings; core-contract reader contract |
| NN/g scanning research (layer-cake pattern; "How Users Read on the Web") | Readers scan headings first; meaningful (not clever or generic) subheadings, one idea per paragraph, and roughly half the conventional word count raise measured usability. | `generic-heading`, `over-sectioned` audit warnings; core-contract surface and proportionality |
| WCAG 2.2 heading and structure guidance | Descriptive headings; consecutive heading levels; meaningful link text; alternative text for informative images. | `heading-level-skip`, `missing-image-alt`, `image-without-context` audit findings; accessibility section of core-contract |
| IBCS SUCCESS formula (IBCS Standards 2.0, aligned with ISO 24896:2026) | One message per chart; assertion titles that state the finding rather than the topic; visual integrity (honest scales); condensed, consistent notation. | `references/modules/visuals.md` charts section; `wide-table` and `table-without-context` audit warnings |
| Assertion-evidence slide design (Alley) | Each slide is one full-sentence assertion supported by visual evidence, not a topic plus bullets. | `references/surfaces/slide.md`; `assets/presentations/academic-talk.html` |
| IMRaD and structured-abstract norms | Question, method, results with uncertainty, and interpretation are distinct roles; claims stay within evidence scope. | `references/modes/experiment-report.md`; `references/modes/academic-synthesis.md`; experiment templates |
| ADR (architecture decision records) | A decision record states status, context, options, decision, and consequences, and is superseded rather than rewritten. | `references/modes/decision-brief.md` decision discipline |
| NeurIPS Paper Checklist; Pineau ML Reproducibility Checklist (JMLR 2021) | Claims match the demonstrated scope; uncertainty statistics are declared with their variability source and run counts; compute and reproduction paths are reported. | `references/modes/experiment-report.md` metric and uncertainty contract, boundary section |
| Error-bar semantics (Cumming, Fidler & Vaux, J Cell Biol 2007) | SD describes spread, SEM/CI support mean comparison; declare the statistic and n; independent repeats only; interval overlap is not a significance test. | `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Beyond bar and line graphs (Weissgerber et al., PLOS Biol 2015) | For small samples, show raw data points instead of summary bars with error bars. | `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Ten simple rules for better figures (Rougier et al., PLOS Comput Biol 2014); figure-legend guidance (Jambor et al., PLOS Biol 2021) | Captions are self-contained and state what, how to read, and the takeaway; figures are cited in prose by their finding, not a bare pointer. | `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Colorblind-safe and perceptually uniform color (Okabe-Ito CUD; Crameri et al., Nat Commun 2020; Borland & Taylor, IEEE CG&A 2007) | Categorical palettes are colorblind-safe; sequential colormaps are perceptually uniform; rainbow/jet colormaps are avoided. | `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Deep RL that Matters (Henderson et al., AAAI 2018); seed-count power analysis (Colas et al. 2018); Statistical Precipice (Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021) | No cherry-picked seeds or best-run curves; seed counts follow expected effect size; learning curves show a declared center and band across seeds with declared smoothing. | `references/modules/ablation.md` reporting; `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Show Your Work (Dodge et al., EMNLP 2019); Are GANs Created Equal? (Lucic et al., NeurIPS 2018) | Comparisons hold tuning budgets symmetric and report budget-dependent results as performance-versus-budget rather than one selected point. | `references/modules/ablation.md` design and reporting |
| Ablation methodology (component interaction and factorial checks; non-additive tricks, Pang et al., ICLR 2021) | Ablations change one thing against the full system, name their form, state the variant tuning policy, and report interactions instead of assuming additivity. | `references/modules/ablation.md` design |
| booktabs table typography (Fear, CTAN) | Print-like tables use horizontal rules only; no vertical or double rules. | `references/modules/tables.md` structure |
| Reproducible LM evaluation (Biderman et al. 2024; HELM, Liang et al. 2022) | Numbers cited from external sources are marked separately from numbers reproduced under the report's protocol; cross-source numbers are not ranked in one comparison. | `references/modules/tables.md` quantitative comparisons |
| ASA p-value statement (Wasserstein & Lazar, TAS 2016); statistical misinterpretation guide (Greenland et al., EJE 2016) | Exact p-values as graded evidence, never a significant/non-significant binary; `p > 0.05` is not proof of no effect; every test pairs with an effect size and interval. | `references/modules/conclusions.md` statistical claims |
| Effect-size reporting (Cohen 1988; Lakens, Front Psychol 2013); absolute-risk communication (Gigerenzer et al., PSPI 2007) | Effect sizes with intervals accompany tests; risks and rates are reported as absolute change with the base rate, not only relative ratios. | `references/modules/conclusions.md` statistical claims |
| Causal language discipline (Hernán, "The C-Word", AJPH 2018) | `associated with`, `predicts`, and causal verbs form a ladder; causal verbs require randomization or a stated identification strategy; no causal advice on associational evidence. | `references/modules/conclusions.md` statistical claims |
| HARKing (Kerr, PSPR 1998); forking paths (Gelman & Loken 2013); multiple-comparison corrections (Holm; Benjamini-Hochberg) | Post-hoc findings are labeled exploratory; the same data cannot generate and confirm a hypothesis; comparison families and corrections are named. | `references/modules/conclusions.md` statistical claims |
| CONSORT 2010 flow diagram; PRISMA 2020 | Every candidate unit is accounted for: counts and reasons at each exclusion step; systematic syntheses disclose search strategy, window, criteria, and screening counts. | `references/modes/experiment-report.md` comparability and selection; `references/modes/academic-synthesis.md` multi-paper synthesis |
| Within-the-bar bias (Newman & Scholl, PB&R 2012); CI overestimation (Hofman, Goldstein & Hullman, CHI 2020); deceptive-visualization taxonomy (Pandey et al., CHI 2015) | Means with uncertainty are points with intervals, not bars with error bars; bars are for counts and true-zero magnitudes with zero-based axes; prediction intervals or distributions for individual-level claims; no dual independently scaled axes. | `references/modules/visuals.md` scientific figures |
| Systems benchmarking crimes (Heiser; van der Kouwe et al., Euro S&P 2019); Fleming & Wallace (CACM 1986); Bailey "Twelve Ways" (1991); The Tail at Scale (Dean & Barroso, CACM 2013) | Full-suite results with cost cases; geometric mean for normalized ratios, harmonic for rates; no `up to N×` alone; baseline in the denominator; tail percentiles beside means; complete platform disclosure. | `references/modules/benchmarking.md` |
| SPEC CPU 2017 run rules; MLCommons/MLPerf submission rules; Green AI (Schwartz et al., CACM 2020) | Institutionalized medians over selected runs; comparable versus open configurations are labeled; reproduction materials accompany results; efficiency and compute cost are reportable first-class metrics. | `references/modules/benchmarking.md` protocol and disclosure |
| Ten simple rules for structuring papers (Mensh & Kording, PLOS Comput Biol 2017) | Context-content-conclusion ordering holds at document, section, and paragraph scale; one central contribution per report. | Semantic order sections of every mode file; core-contract reader contract |
| Presentation design (Doumont, "Trees, maps, and theorems" 2009); assertion-evidence practice | Talks end on a conclusions slide that stays visible through questions; anticipated questions get appendix slides; one message per slide. | `references/surfaces/slide.md` slide contract |
| Peer-review response norms (NeurIPS/ICLR author guides; Parikh rebuttal guidance) | Point-by-point responses quote each comment, lead with the outcome, give precise revision locations, and never promise unverifiable future work. | `assets/templates/rebuttal-response.md` |
| Model Cards (Mitchell et al., FAT* 2019); Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru et al., CACM 2021); FAIR principles (Wilkinson et al., Sci Data 2016); ACM artifact badging | Releases state identity, license, persistent locator, intended and out-of-scope use, provenance with exclusion accounting, disaggregated evaluation, limitations, and reproduction scope. | `assets/templates/release-card.md` |

## Update discipline

When absorbing a new standard, encode the smallest testable rule in the narrowest
location first: a deterministic audit finding when the rule is structural, a mode
or module line when it is judgment guidance, and an on-demand template when it is
a document shape. Always-resident text (adapters, core contract) has strict word
and character budgets that are enforced by tests; prefer on-demand assets. Record
the mapping here with the source named precisely enough to re-check it.
