# SBAR handoff: <one-line situation>

<!-- SBAR (Situation-Background-Assessment-Recommendation) is the standard
escalation and handoff structure from safety-critical operations. Use it when a
reader must act on a live situation: an operational escalation, an on-call
handoff, a cross-team transfer, or an urgent request for a decision. Keep the
whole handoff scannable in under a minute. Remove all comments and any empty
optional section before delivery. -->

## Situation

<!-- REQUIRED: One or two sentences. What is happening right now, for whom, and
why this reaches the reader now. Include an as-of timestamp with timezone when
recency matters. -->

## Background

<!-- REQUIRED: Only the history the reader needs to act: relevant prior state,
recent changes, key configuration, and what has already been tried with its
observed effect. Link detailed logs or dashboards instead of copying them. -->

## Assessment

<!-- REQUIRED: Your judgment, clearly separated from raw data: what you think is
going on, at what severity, and with what trajectory (improving, stable,
worsening). Label suspected causes as suspected; never present temporal
coincidence as a proven cause. -->

## Recommendation

<!-- REQUIRED: The specific action you request or recommend, who should take it,
by when, and the contingency if it cannot happen in that window ("if X is not
possible by T, then Y"). Ask the receiver to confirm what they will do when the
channel supports a read-back. -->
