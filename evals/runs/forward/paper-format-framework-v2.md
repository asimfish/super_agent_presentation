LatentPlan addresses model-based control with an action-conditioned latent transition model that conditions latent-state transitions on actions [P1].

The paper evaluates LatentPlan on two simulated control benchmarks and reports that its mean return is 4.1 points higher than that of a behavioral-cloning baseline across three seeds [P1]. This is the main comparative evidence supplied for the method.

The limitations of the supplied evidence are that the account reports neither a statistical significance test nor a real-robot experiment [P1]. Consequently, the evidence supports only the bounded claim of a higher mean return under these two simulated benchmarks and three-seed evaluation; it does not establish statistical significance, real-robot validation, state-of-the-art performance, or broader generalization.
