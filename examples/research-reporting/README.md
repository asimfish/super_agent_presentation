# Research judgment examples

All facts in this directory are synthetic teaching fixtures. Both drafts in each
pair were written by the maintainer for this change, not generated by a controlled
model trial. No empirical effectiveness, readability, or research claim follows
from the comparison. The complete supplied measurements remain in each report's
appendix; unsupported mechanisms and extra experiments are not reported as facts.

| Fact sheet | Measurement-only draft | Research argument | Judgment to examine |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Transfer](transfer-facts.md) | [Draft](transfer-dump.md) | [Report](transfer-report.md) | A local gain accompanied by a cross-domain loss |
| [Compute](compute-facts.md) | [Draft](compute-dump.md) | [Report](compute-report.md) | A system comparison that does not isolate the component |
| [Uncertainty](null-facts.md) | [Draft](null-dump.md) | [Report](null-report.md) | Similar means that establish neither superiority nor equivalence |

For every pair, ask R1-R5 from `reportctl review-prompt`: research question and
knowledge change, evidence and interpretation, attribution/closest alternative,
discriminating next test when needed, and information hierarchy. Also check every
value, comparator, uncertainty definition, and scope against the fact sheet.

Expected boundary: the dump may pass mechanical audit. It should still need
substantive revision because its measurements do not answer the research question.
The research argument must not be rewarded for length, extra hypotheses, fabricated
thresholds, or a more positive conclusion. Negative and unresolved outcomes are
intentional.

Example commands, from the repository root:

```bash
python3 skills/agentic-reporting/scripts/reportctl.py audit \
  --file examples/research-reporting/transfer-report.md --mode experiment-report
python3 skills/agentic-reporting/scripts/reportctl.py review-prompt \
  --file examples/research-reporting/transfer-report.md \
  --facts examples/research-reporting/transfer-facts.md --mode experiment-report
```

Give the review prompt to an independent reviewer. Check actual findings rather
than treating the presence of R1-R5 keywords as verification of scientific quality.

The [recorded cross-model semantic pass](semantic-review.json) binds the three
pairs to file hashes. The reviewer marked all measurement-only drafts for
substantive revision and found no remaining major/blocking issue in the research
arguments. Two initial rule/exemplar findings were corrected and rechecked.
This is a fixture review, not a blinded effectiveness comparison.
